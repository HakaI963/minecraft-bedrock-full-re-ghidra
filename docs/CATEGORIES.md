# Categories

`Block & World`, `Item & Inventory`, `Entity & Mob`, `UI & Main Menu`, `Networking`,
`Rendering & Graphics`, `Audio`, `Physics & Math`, `Storage & NBT`,
`Redstone & Mechanisms`, `Input & Controls`, `Character & Skin`, `Security & DRM`,
`Runtime & OS`, `Other / Uncategorised`.

A category report lists each entry's kind (function/symbol/string), its file offset or
virtual address, the recovered name, the signature/section and the size/vaddr. Reports are
generated mechanically by `scripts/categorize.py` from the full exports - nothing is
hand-picked or dropped.
